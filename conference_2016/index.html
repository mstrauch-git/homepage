The p-adic Langlands Program and Related Topics
May 16-20, 2016

Indiana University Bloomington    

The organizers gratefully acknowledge the financial support of the National Science Foundation, the Institute for Advanced Study at Indiana University, and the Department of Mathematics of Indiana University through funds from the Institute for Mathematics and its Applications.

The purpose of the conference is to bring together experts working in the p-adic Langlands Program and related areas, and to provide an overview of current research pursued in fields like: representations of p-adic reductive groups in characteristic p, p-adic Banach space representations and locally analytic representations, p-adic Galois representations and their deformation spaces, p-adic Hodge Theory, (φ,Γ)-modules, eigenvarieties, and p-adic automorphic forms.

Accommodation and Travel Support:

Thanks to the financial support of the NSF and the Institute for Advanced Study at IU, we plan to reimburse the travel or lodging expenses of as many participants as our funding allows. The support of graduate students and recent PhDs will receive priority. If you are interested in participating in the conference, please e-mail one of the organizers (scroll down to see organizer e-mail addresses) with your institutional affiliation, your advisor's name if you are a graduate student, and whether you wish to request support for travel or lodging.

Dining and Coffee:

  • Breaks:  There will be a number of breaks in between talks during each day of the conference during which beverages including coffee and tea will be provided. More substantial refreshments including cookies and pastries will be provided during the longer breaks.
  • Restaurants and Coffee: There are many restaurants near the university offering a wide variety of cuisine including Afghan, Burmese, Eritrean, Indian, Moroccan, Thai, and Tibetan. Most have fare amenable to vegetarian diets, and one can find a decent cup of coffee in town.
  • Conference dinner:  There will be a conference dinner buffet on Wednesday, May 18, at Samira's restaurant. The costs of $18 per person are to be paid in cash at registration or afterwards to the organizers.
